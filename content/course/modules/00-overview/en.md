# Course Overview

Sprintest is a practical QA course built for learning with an AI tutor. The course gives structure, short lessons, realistic scenarios, practice tasks, review criteria, and hint rules. The tutor helps you reason, asks questions, and reviews your artifacts.

The important part is that you still do the QA work. You decide what is risky, what is unclear, which checks are useful, and what evidence would help a team make a release decision.

## What you will learn

You will build the core habits of a junior QA engineer:

- ask useful questions before testing;
- read requirements critically;
- plan checks around risk, value, and constraints;
- design test ideas, checklists, test cases, and bug reports;
- explain testing results in a way a team can act on.

The full course covers QA foundations, SDLC, testing types and levels, test design, web and API basics, SQL, logs, test management, communication, and interview practice.

## How to learn with an AI tutor

1. Ask the tutor to load the course manifest and the module you are studying.
2. Read one short section at a time.
3. Answer check questions in your own words.
4. Complete the practice task before requesting examples.
5. Revise your artifact after review.

Treat the tutor as a reviewer and coach, not as a replacement for your thinking. If an answer feels too complete too early, ask for questions or hints and make your own attempt first.

## What good QA work looks like

Good QA is not only finding bugs. It includes asking precise questions, noticing risk, choosing useful tests, and explaining evidence clearly enough for a team to act.

In this course, open artifacts can be written as prose, tables, checklists, test cases, or mixed notes. The tutor reviews the thinking and usefulness of the artifact, not a rigid template match.
